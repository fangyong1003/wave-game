# 《异常房屋维修员》概念图生成记录

用途：开发前确认冷峻悬疑、半写实画面的方向；不是实际游戏截图。

生成方式：内置 image_gen 工具，通过 imagegen 技能组织提示词。

用户已选择的风格：冷峻悬疑，空楼、冷光、克制的紧张感。

用户已查看生成结果并反馈：“喜欢这种写实风格”。最终以生成图中的写实表现为准，历史提示词中的 semi-realistic 保留为生成记录，不作为后续降低写实程度的要求。

## 完整生成提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: first-person video game art direction concept, one coherent widescreen image, 16:9.
Primary request: Establish a restrained cold suspense visual direction for an original 3D first-person game called "Anomalous Apartment Maintenance". The player is a municipal repair technician investigating an empty apartment block where domestic physics is broken. This is a proposed art target, not a screenshot of an existing game.
Scene: Eye-level view from the doorway of a compact, plausible aging Chinese urban apartment kitchenette, approximately 20 square meters with connected corridor glimpsed behind an open door. Desaturated celadon ceramic tiles halfway up plaster walls, scratched painted cabinetry, worn terrazzo floor, stainless sink, an unbranded enamel cup, exposed water pipes, frosted rain-streaked window. Human-scale believable architecture and subtle signs of past habitation, no occupants.
Signature anomaly: a thin sharply readable stream of water travels UPWARD from the sink faucet basin area in a graceful unnatural arc toward a small dark seam at the ceiling. A few separated beads of water hang along its upward path. One small enamel cup is hovering only 10 cm above the counter. Most of the room remains ordinary and grounded.
Gameplay readability: At the right wall, an OPEN utility service panel reveals a visually simple three-way junction of pipes, one large handwheel, one red-capped bypass fitting and a small transparent collection canister mounted below it. These should look like practical hand-accessible maintenance hardware, not science fiction. A small warm amber work light illuminates the service panel, making it the natural secondary focal point.
First person: Only a little of the technician's blue-gray work-jacket sleeves and plain dark work gloves visible along the bottom edge. Left hand holds a compact scratched analog diagnostic gauge with an off-center needle, right hand loosely holds a recognizable ordinary adjustable wrench, low in frame, not raised as a weapon. Hands occupy less than 15 percent of image height. Central view remains unobstructed.
Style/medium: polished semi-realistic 3D game concept render with simplified clean geometry, carefully chosen believable PBR materials, restrained detail density, feasible for an indie Blender asset workflow. Visually legible surfaces and strong composition rather than photoreal clutter. No heavy outlines, no pixelation, no PS1/PSX dithering.
Lighting/mood: overcast rainy blue-gray ambient daylight through window, subtle cold fluorescent kitchen strip, single very small amber practical utility lamp. Moody and quiet with visible midtones and readable corners. Tension comes from the impossible water motion and the empty domestic environment.
Color palette: slate blue #27343B, faded celadon #667875, plaster off-white #B7BDB6, muted rust red #92574C, tiny amber #C99A53 accents. The palette must feel cold and mundane, not teal-orange cinematic.
Composition: natural rectilinear lens equivalent to roughly 70-degree game view, clear leading lines, sink and upward stream in middle-left, maintenance panel middle-right, subtle corridor depth, realistic first-person height. No collage or split panels.
Constraints: no monsters, blood, gore, occult symbols, weapons, jumpscare figure, neon sci-fi lighting, branded props, visible people, giant glowing cracks, combat UI, title typography, captions, watermark or logos. This is one understated gameplay-view art reference. Do not make room completely dark.
```
