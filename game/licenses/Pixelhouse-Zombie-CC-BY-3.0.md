The source page explicitly identifies the asset license as CC BY 3.0. The original archive and its `read first.txt` are preserved in `art/external/pixelhouse_zombie/`.

Modifications in this project: converted FBX to a Blender source and glTF binary, combined walk/fury/dead animations on one skeleton, renamed clips, normalized scene scale and orientation, reassigned diffuse and normal maps, tuned roughness and normal intensity, created three runtime color/build variations, and connected animation playback to the game AI. The source's specular map is retained in the source folder but not used at runtime. These modifications do not imply endorsement by Pixelhouse.
